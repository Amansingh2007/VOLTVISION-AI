Smart Energy Optimization & Tariff Rules

Actionable, data-backed strategies tailored to Indian DISCOM and international utility standards.

❄️

Air Conditioner Mastery

Every 1°C increase in your AC set-temperature (e.g. from 20°C to 24°C) saves roughly 6% of the compressor's electricity. Use ceiling fans simultaneously for optimal circulation.

Saves ₹400 – ₹900/mo
⏰

Time-Of-Day (ToD) Tariff Shifting

Avoid running heavy inductive loads (washing machines, geysers, EV charging) during peak grid stress between 6:00 PM and 10:00 PM when peak surcharges apply.

Saves ₹250 – ₹600/mo
☀️

Net Metering & Solar Rooftop

A 2 kW solar rooftop setup generates 8–10 units daily, cutting typical 2-3 BHK bills by up to 70%. Surplus energy can be sold back to the grid via net metering.

Saves ₹1,800 – ₹3,000/mo
🔌

Eliminate Phantom / Standby Loads

TVs, microwaves, set-top boxes, and laptop chargers consume 5W–15W continuously when left plugged in. Smart surge strips eliminate this invisible drain.

Saves ₹120 – ₹250/mo
🌀

Switch to BLDC Inverter Fans

Standard induction fans consume 75W, while Brushless DC (BLDC) motor fans consume only 28W–32W with silent operation and multi-speed remotes.

Saves ₹300 – ₹500/mo
🧼

Washing Machine Cold Wash

90% of a washing machine's power goes towards heating water. Using cold wash cycles (30°C or normal tap temperature) drastically reduces cycle consumption.

Saves ₹150 – ₹350/mo
